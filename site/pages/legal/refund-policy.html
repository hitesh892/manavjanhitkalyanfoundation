Refund Policy

We aim to process every contribution accurately and transparently. This policy explains when a donation refund may be considered and how to request a review.

01

General Policy

Donations are voluntary contributions intended to support our charitable activities. A completed donation is not automatically refundable, but each genuine request is reviewed fairly.

02

When a Refund May Be Considered

We may review duplicate payments, technical errors, an incorrect amount caused by an apparent transaction issue, or an unauthorised transaction reported promptly.

03

Requests We May Not Approve

We may be unable to refund a valid, completed donation where there is no processing error or other reasonable basis. Payment-provider rules and applicable law may also limit available remedies.

04

How to Request a Review

Email info@manavjanhit.org with your name, transaction reference, date, amount and reason. Do not send card numbers, passwords or other sensitive credentials by email.

05

Review and Processing Time

We aim to acknowledge requests and review them within a reasonable period. If approved, the refund is sent through the original payment method where possible; the final credit timing depends on the payment provider or bank.

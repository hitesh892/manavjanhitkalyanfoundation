Our CSR focus areas

Focused Today. A Kinder Tomorrow.

We work across key areas where support can bring immediate relief and long-term dignity.

Elderly Care & Shelter

Support for senior citizens through care, shelter and a dignified living environment.

Ambulance Assistance

Timely medical transport support for people in need, especially the vulnerable.

Dignified Last Rites

Support for respectful and dignified final rites for people without adequate assistance.

Food & Community Support

Nutritious food and community support for children, families and people in crisis.
